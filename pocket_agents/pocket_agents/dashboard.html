How the score works. Each ETF gets an opportunity score from −100 (stretched / trim) to +100 (oversold / accumulate), blending three mean-reversion cues: 14-day RSI (45%), distance from its 50-day moving average (30%), and where it sits in its 52-week range (25%). Higher = cheaper relative to its own recent history.
Prices are end-of-day from the pocket-fetch pipeline (, ASX close). Educational tool for spotting mean-reversion setups across your own watchlist — not financial advice. Technical signals describe the past; they don't predict returns. Do your own research.
